Table terms

Diamonds are the marked dots on the rails. They are about a foot apart on a 9-foot table. A hand-span is about the same distance.

Head rail is the short rail at the end you break from. Foot rail is the one at the other end. The head spot and foot spot are the dots a quarter of the way in from each of those rails. The head string is the line across the table through the head spot.

These setups work on any table size. Move the balls a little closer together on a smaller table.
